Venus

About Venus

Venus is the second planet from the Sun and is similar in size to Earth. It has a thick atmosphere containing mainly carbon dioxide.

Interesting Facts

  • Venus is the hottest planet in the Solar System.
  • It has a thick atmosphere and clouds of sulfuric acid.
  • Venus rotates in the opposite direction to most planets.
  • A day on Venus is longer than a year on Venus.
